PROJECT INFORMATION

PuriAir

An IoT Multi-Classroom Air Quality Analytics Platform with Occupancy-Driven Predictive Purification

PuriAir is designed to provide real-time environmental visibility, predictive intelligence, and operational awareness for indoor classroom environments.

PUPCollege of Engineering
Computer Engineering
01

Real-Time Environmental Visibility

Continuously monitors and visualizes CO₂, PM2.5, temperature, relative humidity, and classroom environmental status through live telemetry.

  • Live sensor telemetry
  • Classroom Health Index (CHI)
  • Multi-classroom environmental overview
02

Occupancy-Driven Predictive Analytics

Uses classroom occupancy and environmental trends to anticipate near-term pollutant accumulation and potential air-quality deterioration.

  • PIR-based occupancy context
  • Historical trend analysis
  • 15-minute forecasting
03

Automated Purification

Supports automated relay-based purification responses when environmental conditions require intervention, while providing real-time operational feedback.

  • Purifier state monitoring
  • Automated control logic
  • Manual administrator override
04

Decision Support

Transforms environmental data into actionable alerts and recommendations for administrators, faculty, and staff.

  • Threshold alerts
  • Predictive warnings
  • Air-management recommendations
